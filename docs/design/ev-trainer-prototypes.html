MeowRPGAdvisor · design prototypes · 2026-11-28

EV Trainer for team & box

Prototypes for a new feature: which EVs each Pokémon should grind, for the whole box and the team, by play style. A second part has three more feature ideas with their own mocks. Every mock is interactive and uses invented sample Pokémon, not anyone's real box.

Research

What already exists, and what's missing

Already in 0.15.0

Prep → Team → EVs → on each team member. It scores 6–8 common 252/252 spreads (8 for mixed attackers) against one NPC, lists what to raise or lower, and where to farm (vitamins, Power items, wild Pokémon, NPC rematches, training accounts). Code: evPlan.ts, EvPanel.tsx.

The gap

  • It only appears after you pick an NPC, and only for the 6 suggested Pokémon.
  • No idea of style: a Snorlax can be a slow bulky hitter or a special wall.
  • No box view, so you can't plan farm sessions across several Pokémon.
  • No overview of what's done, what's wrong (510 used on the wrong stats) and what it costs.

Game EV rules (wiki, read 2026-11-23)

  • Max 252 per stat, 510 total. 4 EVs = 1 point at Lv 100 (about half that at Lv 50).
  • From wild, trainer and NPC battles. None from World Bosses, the Frontier, live PvP or Quick catch.
  • Power item +8 per KO. Macho Brace doubles. Vitamin +10. EV Berry −10. Fresh-Start Mochi resets all six.
  • Rematch NPCs have 31 IVs and 508 EVs. Rivals are Lv 120 with trained EVs.

What "style" should mean in this game

On competitive sites a style is a role in PvP (sweeper, wall, Trick Room). Most battles here are against NPCs, so a style combines a role (how the Pokémon fights) with a goal (which battles it trains for). Roles come from base stats and moves, so they cost almost nothing to compute for a whole box. Goals reuse the existing scorer (evPlanFor) on a set of NPCs. That's accurate but expensive, so it runs only when you ask.

Smogon spreads: an optional, unverified source not recommended for v1

The pkmn project publishes Smogon sets as JSON (data.pkmn.cc, which now redirects to pkmn.github.io/smogon/data/). I couldn't read its README or confirm the file shape this session: one fetch returned 503, the other 404. Costs: sets written in Smogon analyses are copyrighted by Smogon (only sets derived from usage stats are MIT), so they can't go in this public repo; fetching them needs a new host permission in the manifest; and they're tuned for PvP at Lv 50/100 rather than for NPC fights at the game's level caps. If you want it later, add it as a "Smogon reference" line on the Build card that's fetched on Sync, never stored in the repo.

Placement

Where it lives: a new Train tab

BattleWildPrepTrainGuide· ⚙ Settings stays on the right

Prep is a flow that starts with "pick an NPC", but box-wide EV planning isn't about one NPC. A tab of its own keeps Prep short and gives the box a home. Prep → Team keeps its EVs → link, which now opens the Build card (Prototype B) with the goal set to that NPC. The 0.15.0 logic is reused, not thrown away. One alternative is a 4th Prep step, "Train". It's cheaper to build, but it inherits the NPC gate, so I don't recommend it.

Prototype A · Train → Box

EV Board: the whole box at a glance

Why

The main question is "who should I train next, and in what?", and it's about many Pokémon at once. A scannable list with progress bars answers that faster than opening cards one by one. It also catches mistakes, such as a Gengar with 252 Attack.

What

Each row shows six EV bars (fill = now, black tick = target), a status pill and what's left to grind. Chips at the top change the role for every row. A row's own role can be changed in its Build card.

  • Filters: Team (the carried 6) · Needs work · Lv 30+. Sorted by "least left first", so quick wins come first.
  • States: Done Grind Reset first (510 used on the wrong stats) EVs unknown (typed by hand) Later (under Lv 25, 252 EVs add 15 points or fewer). Natures that lower the role's attack stat get a warning line.
  • Goal mode ("League rematches" chip): rows aren't scored until you tap one. That's honest about the cost.
How
  • Reads the box already stored in IndexedDB (meowdex, key box), where EVs, IVs, nature and level are already synced. No new game requests.
  • Roles: a new pure function roleFor(species, moves) using attackSide() from evPlan.ts plus base stats. No damage calc, so it should stay cheap for hundreds of Pokémon (not measured yet).
  • Goal scores: evPlanFor × the goal's NPC teams, run on tap and cached per (Pokémon, goal) until the next Box Sync. scoreAgainstTeam runs a full calc duel per pair, so scoring 300 Pokémon × 8 spreads × 12 teams live would freeze the panel.
  • The role choice per Pokémon is stored in a new key, mdEvStyle. Dataset doesn't change, so there's no SCHEMA_VERSION bump.

Prototype B · Train → one Pokémon

Build card: compare styles by what they buy

Why

"252 Atk / 252 Spe" means little on its own. People decide when they see the result: "outspeeds Weavile", "survives Earthquake". You also asked for different maximized EVs depending on style. Comparing them side by side, each with its consequences, makes that choice concrete instead of a label.

What
  • Style tabs: Fast attacker · Bulky attacker · Lean. Stat bars show now (grey) against the style (blue) at the fight's level.
  • "What it buys" against the chosen goal: speed order is computed live (mainline formula). Survival lines come from the calc.
  • Warnings: a nature that works against the style, 510 already full (berries vs Mochi), and "too low for EVs to matter".
  • The farm plan for this Pokémon reuses the 0.15.0 "where to farm" list.
How
  • Stats: the existing boxToCalc with real IVs and nature. Speed lines: compare against the goal's NPC sets (rematch NPCs at 508 EVs: their spread isn't known, so it shows a range, and that's a guess).
  • Lean: the smallest Spe EVs (a multiple of 4, at the fight's level) that move first against the chosen threat. The rest goes to the attack stat, then HP.
  • Opens from Box rows, Team rows and Prep → Team → EVs (goal = that NPC).

Prototype C · Train → Farm

Farm session: grouped by stat, not by Pokémon

Why

Grinding is done one stat at a time: you fight Speed-yield Pokémon in one area for a while. Three Pokémon that each need Speed can train together (with Exp. Share holders), so a per-Pokémon list hides the cheapest plan. This view turns the box plan into sessions and a shopping list.

What
  • One block per stat: total needed, who needs it, and the ways to get it, cheapest first (training account, wild spot, vitamins).
  • "Bring": who battles and who holds Exp. Share, with the right Power item on each.
  • A shopping list against the bag (vitamins, berries, Power items) shows what you have and what to buy.
  • It updates from Prototype A: change the role chip there and these totals change.
How
  • Sums the per-Pokémon gaps from A. The farm options are the existing wildEvSpots, npcEvBattles, trainingAccounts and koCount.
  • The bag comes from mdBag (Bag reader). Ticks are stored in a new local key, mdEvQueue. Nothing is clicked in the game.
  • Guesses to label: Exp. Share holders get the same EVs (as the current tip says), and a Power item gives +8 per KO the holder takes part in.

More ideas to grow the extension

Three extras, each read-only and advice-only

All of them read data the extension already has or a screen it already reads. None clicks or types in the game, injects UI into the game page or sends a request outside a Sync button.

Idea 1 · Prep → Team

Speed Ladder

Why

Most of the 0.16.0 advice comes down to "who moves first". It's also the main question for EV choices (Prototype B). A ladder makes the speed order visible for a fight and shows exactly what a few Spe EVs would change.

What

Your team and the NPC's at the fight level, sorted by Speed. Dashed rungs show "with +N Spe EVs" ghosts where a small amount of EVs passes a foe. Tapping a ghost opens the Build card in Lean mode.

How

Speed from boxToCalc/npcToCalc. No new data, no schema change. Guesses: NPC EV spreads (shown as a range for rematch NPCs), Choice Scarf/paralysis only when seen. Mainline speed ties are random, which the ladder marks with "tie".

Idea 2 · Battle tab, after the battle

Battle Debrief

Why

Today the advice disappears when the battle ends. After a loss, people want to know what decided it and what to change: levels, EVs, a move, or the lead. The battle reader already logs every turn, so the debrief can point at the 1–2 turns that mattered.

What

Result, then 2–3 "turning points" (outsped by 17, a 2HKO that became an OHKO after a crit, a switch that was scored badly), each with one fix that links somewhere (Build card, Grind levels, moves). It also lists damage checks that were "off", which hint that the opponent's set differs from the wiki.

How

mdBattle holds only the latest screen, so the content script would also copy the final snapshot to a new key, mdLastBattle (no renames). Turning points: replay the log through damageCheck and the lookahead score swing per turn. No game requests. "Would have won with X" is a model estimate and must be labeled as one.

Idea 3 · Wild / Explore tab

Session Route: one map, three goals

Why

Hunt list, EV farming and level grinding currently send you to different maps. Since time is what people spend, a map that serves all three in one session beats three separate trips.

What

The top 3 reachable maps, each scored by what it covers: catches from the hunt list, EVs from the Farm queue, EXP for the next Grind level target. Each shows what to KO, what to catch and what to hold.

How

Joins mdHunt, the Farm queue (C), wildEvSpots and the grind plan over the wiki map encounter tables already in the Dataset. Reachability reuses the Hunt & Grind gate guess (already in "Needs real-game check"). Maps only, no exact parts or time of day. No new requests and no schema bump.
